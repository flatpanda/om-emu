Scene and image sensor (S-SCENE stand-in)

The camera's sensor, image pipeline, AF and release are not modelled. This stand-in renders a synthetic scene (layers at three distances, a thin lens on a Four Thirds sensor, the exposure triangle) into the live-view bank, and the firmware's own through-image API shows it on the IMG1 plane (it needs A15). S1 focuses on the layer under the AF point S2 records a capture with the exposure in use and REC starts and ends a clip: all three are the stand-in's, not the firmware's. The firmware's camera application answers S1 and S2 itself (the date prompt, the shooting information, the focus confirmation, the capture display for the shutter time in use, the rec view), with the modules' notifications of a release stood in (S-RELSEQ). REC records with the mode dial at Movie (position 7) after the LLPSW firmware key (Firmware keys, 1.1.4 0x121c) is released; with LLPSW pressed, as the lab's idle image has it, the application refuses the key's function.

Subject detection: the scene's subjects are its patterns' (the moving scene's rider stands for a motorsport subject); the scene takes no subjects of its own at run time. The camera's own setting chooses the kind (id 1682, 1 motorsports to 5 animals: its menu, or the debug API's token SET:1682=1), and while it names the kind of a subject the live view shows, DISP_FRAME draws the frames natively from the boxes S-SUBJECT writes where CPU 3's MDET would.
